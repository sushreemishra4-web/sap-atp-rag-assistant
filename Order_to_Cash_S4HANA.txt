SAP S/4HANA Order-to-Cash (O2C) Process
==========================================

1. Overview
-----------
Order-to-Cash (O2C) is the end-to-end business process that starts when a customer places an order and ends when payment is received and cleared.

In SAP S/4HANA, the process integrates Sales and Distribution (SD) with Inventory Management, Logistics Execution, Transportation where applicable, Billing, Accounts Receivable (FI-AR), and Credit Management.

Typical flow:

Customer Inquiry/Quotation
        |
        v
Sales Order
        |
        v
Delivery
        |
        v
Picking / Packing / Goods Issue
        |
        v
Billing
        |
        v
Accounts Receivable
        |
        v
Incoming Payment / Clearing

2. Pre-Sales Activities
-----------------------
The process may begin with:
- Inquiry
- Quotation
- Customer-specific pricing
- Availability checks
- Customer and material determination

A quotation can be converted into a sales order if the customer accepts the offer.

3. Sales Order
--------------
The sales order captures the customer's commercial requirements.

Important data includes:
- Sold-to party
- Ship-to party
- Bill-to party
- Payer
- Material
- Quantity
- Plant
- Requested delivery date
- Pricing
- Taxes
- Payment terms
- Incoterms
- Shipping conditions

During order processing, SAP can perform:
- Availability/ATP checks
- Pricing determination
- Partner determination
- Credit checks
- Delivery scheduling
- Output/message determination

The result is an order containing the commercial and logistical requirements for fulfilment.

4. Delivery Processing
----------------------
The outbound delivery controls the logistics execution of the sales order.

Typical steps:
1. Delivery creation
2. Picking
3. Packing, if applicable
4. Transportation/shipping activities
5. Post Goods Issue (PGI)

PGI confirms that the goods have left the company's inventory. It normally triggers the relevant inventory and accounting updates.

5. Billing
----------
After the required logistics steps are completed, the delivery can be billed.

Common billing types include:
- Invoice
- Credit memo
- Debit memo
- Pro forma invoice, where applicable

Billing copies relevant data from the preceding document according to copy-control settings.

During billing, SAP determines:
- Billing date
- Pricing
- Taxes
- Payment terms
- Accounting data
- Customer receivable
- Revenue and other relevant G/L postings

The billing document normally creates an FI accounting document.

6. Accounts Receivable and Payment
----------------------------------
Once billing is posted, the customer has an open receivable.

The customer then makes payment. The incoming payment is posted in FI-AR and cleared against the relevant open invoice(s).

The final stage is therefore:

Invoice
   |
   v
Open Customer Item
   |
   v
Incoming Payment
   |
   v
Cleared Item

7. Integration Points
---------------------
O2C is highly integrated across SAP:

SD:
- Sales orders
- Deliveries
- Shipping
- Billing
- Pricing

MM/Inventory:
- Stock availability
- Goods issue
- Material movements

FI:
- Revenue posting
- Tax
- Accounts receivable
- Incoming payment
- Clearing

FSCM Credit Management:
- Credit limit
- Credit exposure
- Credit checks
- Credit blocks/releases

TM, EWM and other solutions may also participate depending on the S/4HANA landscape.

8. Important Document Flow
--------------------------
A standard make-to-stock sales flow can look like:

Quotation
   ↓
Sales Order
   ↓
Outbound Delivery
   ↓
Picking/Packing
   ↓
Post Goods Issue
   ↓
Billing Document
   ↓
FI Accounting Document
   ↓
Customer Payment
   ↓
Clearing

SAP document flow allows users to trace the relationship between these documents and investigate issues across the process.

9. Common O2C Issues
--------------------
Typical support issues include:
- Incorrect or missing pricing
- Wrong partner determination
- ATP/availability failure
- Delivery block
- Credit block
- Incomplete sales order
- Picking or PGI failure
- Billing block
- Incorrect copy-control behaviour
- Tax determination errors
- Currency differences
- FI posting errors
- Customer payment or clearing issues

For troubleshooting, start with the document flow and identify the first point where the expected data or status changes. Then analyse the relevant master data, configuration, determination procedure and enhancement.

10. Key Success Factors
-----------------------
A reliable O2C process requires:
- Correct customer/BP master data
- Correct material and plant data
- Robust pricing and tax determination
- Accurate partner determination
- Correct ATP and delivery settings
- Proper credit management
- Correct copy control
- Reliable FI integration
- Clear exception handling

The strength of S/4HANA O2C is the integration of commercial, logistics and financial processes into a connected end-to-end flow, giving businesses better visibility from customer demand through fulfilment, billing and cash collection.
